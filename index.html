UNDER REVIEW

StereoHOI: Recover Metric Hand-Object Interaction from Egocentric Stereo Vision

Qiwei Wang, Xiangting Meng, Xingxing Zuo, Yujiao Shi

Abstract +

Recovers metric-scale 3D hand-object interaction from egocentric stereo images: fuses frozen hand-foundation-model priors with camera parameters, models stereo disparity via alternating cross-view attention, and predicts metric wrist translation and pose residuals, placing hand and object meshes in one metric frame.

NEURIPS 2026

Seeing Across Skies and Streets: Feedforward 3D Reconstruction from Satellite, Drone, and Ground Images

Qiwei Wang, Zhongyao Tuo, Xianghui Ze, Yujiao Shi

Paper ↗ Code ↗
Abstract +

Cross3R, a feedforward model that jointly processes satellite, drone, and ground images to directly regress full 3D structure and 6-DoF poses, beyond prior planar position and yaw estimation; also builds CrossGeo (85 locations worldwide, 278K images) and outperforms specialized methods zero-shot on KITTI.

ICLR 2026

CylinderSplat: 3D Gaussian Splatting with Cylindrical Triplanes for Panoramic Novel View Synthesis

Qiwei Wang, Xianghui Ze, Jingyi Yu, Yujiao Shi

Paper ↗ Code ↗
Abstract +

A feedforward 3D Gaussian Splatting framework that tackles the distortion and occlusion of equirectangular panoramas with a cylindrical triplane representation, decoupling azimuth and elevation to improve geometric consistency; achieves SOTA on single- and multi-view panoramic novel view synthesis.

NEURIPS 2025 · SPOTLIGHT

BevSplat: Resolving Height Ambiguity via Feature-Based Gaussian Primitives for Weakly-Supervised Cross-View Localization

Qiwei Wang, Shaoxun Wu, Yujiao Shi

Paper ↗ Code ↗
Abstract +

Weakly supervised cross-view localization that resolves ground-satellite height ambiguity: feature-based 3D Gaussian Splatting synthesizes geometrically consistent BEV features from ground images, aligned with satellite features to solve for pose; significantly outperforms prior weakly supervised methods on KITTI and VIGOR.

ICLR 2025

Controllable Satellite-to-Street-View Synthesis with Precise Pose Alignment and Zero-Shot Environmental Control

Xianghui Ze, Zhenbo Song, Qiwei Wang, Jianfeng Lu, Yujiao Shi

Paper ↗ Code ↗
Abstract +

A diffusion-based method combining iterative homography adjustment with text-guided sampling for satellite-to-street-view synthesis, achieving zero-shot pose control and fine-grained control of weather, lighting, and season without target-scene data.
